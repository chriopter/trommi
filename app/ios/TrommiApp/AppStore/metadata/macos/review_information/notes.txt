What Trommi does: it is the Mac and phone side of coding agents (Claude Code, Codex) that people run on their own Linux or macOS computer. When an agent needs a decision, it sends a "card" with a question and a few options; the person answers with one click and the agent continues. Cards, chats, notes and files are end-to-end encrypted on the devices (MLS); our server only relays sealed envelopes.

How to review: sign in with the account given here (start screen: "Log in", then the e-mail and the password). It opens a filled workspace: the Desk with open questions, cards with pictures, chats with agent sessions and their helpers, notes and Settings. Its agents are not running on a computer at the moment, so the sessions show "gone" and an answer waits for them; everything else works as for any user. The data in it is made up.

Without the account: on the start screen, click "Try the demo" (at the foot of the window). It opens the same made-up workspace ("Fernly") on the device, with nothing sent to a server and nothing stored; "Leave" in the yellow Demo tag returns to the start screen, and "All Screens" in the tag lists every screen of the app.

A new account: "Create Account" with any e-mail address and a password works and needs no confirmation e-mail. A new account has an empty Desk until an agent is connected: connecting one needs a computer with Claude Code or Codex and the Trommi connector (instructions at https://trommi.com).

Account deletion: Settings > Account > "Delete Account…" (asks for the password, then deletes the account and all of its data on the server).

Push notifications are optional and asked for after sign-in; the app works fully without them. The Mac app does not use the camera: adding it to an account by link uses "Paste a link" on the start screen. The open questions are also listed in the menu bar (the bell); a click opens the card in the window.
