Your agents ring. Answer anywhere.

Claude Code and Codex ask as cards on your phone. One tap, and they keep working. End-to-end encrypted.

Coding agents stop when they need you: a design choice, a permission, a question only you can answer. Trommi brings that question to your pocket, so the work goes on while you are away from your computer.

AGENTS ASK, YOU DECIDE
A session files a card with its question and a few options, with pictures or a page to try when it helps. One tap answers, and the agent carries on.

ONE LIST PER GROUP
Your chats and teams, grouped. Tap a group and its open cards stand there chat by chat: each chat's questions first, then what is to read. Answer one, and the next card is already open. Blitz walks the open cards of every group, one after the other.

A PUSH WHEN ONE NEEDS YOU
Blocking questions come first and ring; the rest waits quietly in its group. Choose per phone: every question, only the urgent ones, or none.

THE WHOLE CONVERSATION
Chat with any session, see what it is working on, hand a card back with a note, approve a permission request.

NOTES AND THE SCRIBBLE BOARD
Jot a note or draw on the Scribble Board, then send what you selected to an agent.

END-TO-END ENCRYPTED
Cards, answers, chats, drawings and files are encrypted on your devices with MLS, the IETF standard for group messaging. The server delivers sealed envelopes and holds no key that opens them. App, connector and server are open code under the O'Saasy license: read it, change it, host it yourself.

HOW IT CONNECTS
Trommi works with agents you run yourself. On a Linux or macOS machine with Claude Code or Codex, install the Trommi connector once, then connect a session with the invite link from "Invite Agent…" in the app. Compare six emoji, confirm, and the session's next question lands in its group. Instructions: trommi.com

Want to look around first? "Try the demo" on the start screen opens a full made-up workspace on your phone; nothing is sent.
