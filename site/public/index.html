Away from the terminal

Your agents keep working where they run. Trommi brings you only what needs you, on the phone in your pocket, and carries your answer back.

  1. 1It rings

    A session that needs a decision files a card. Your phone gets a push; on an iPhone a Live Activity counts along.

  2. 2You answer

    The question, why it matters now, a few options with pictures. One tap decides, or ask back first.

  3. 3It works on

    The answer reaches the session that asked. Follow the work, chat with it, send it notes and pictures.

Sealed on your devices

Every agent session is its own MLS group (RFC 9420): your devices and that agent, nobody else. Each agent joins only after you compared six emoji in the terminal and in the app.

Encrypted before it leaves a device

  • Cards, options and your answers
  • Chats, notes, drawings and files
  • The names of sessions, desks and devices
  • The keys: the server holds none that opens anything

What the server sees to deliver

  • Your email address and your devices’ public keys
  • Per envelope: sender, recipient, time and size
  • Whether a card is open and how urgent it is
  • Whether an agent is connected

Not audited yet: an independent review of the cryptography is still to come. How it works is in the README; all of the code is there under the O’Saasy license, to read, change and host yourself.

Agents

The connector is one small program on the machine where your agents work, on Linux or macOS. It carries their questions to your board, encrypted, and your answers back.

  • Claude Code

    A plugin: cards, chat, pictures, and a status line that shows the board.

  • Codex

    An MCP server with hooks: Codex asks and reads your answers the same way.

  • More to come

    The connector speaks MCP, the open protocol agents share. Others follow.

Connect an agent
  1. # 1. once per machine: install the connector 
    $ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trommi/trommi/main/install.sh | sh
  2. # 2. in a project folder, start claude (or codex) and paste, with the invite link from Invite Agent… in the app 
    > Connect to Trommi with this invite link: 'the invite link'
    # or in Claude Code, the slash command 
    > /trommi:connect 'the invite link'
  3. # 3. compare the six emoji here and in the app, confirm: the next question lands on your Desk

Claude Code asks once whether to use the trommi MCP server: choose “Use this MCP server”.

Questions

What people ask before they connect their first agent.

My agent has a remote control built in. Why Trommi?
A built-in remote control mirrors one maker’s sessions. Trommi puts Claude Code and Codex on one Desk, brings you the questions instead of the whole terminal, and seals everything end to end.
Can I start a new session from my phone?
Not yet. A session starts on your machine and is connected once; from then on everything it asks reaches you wherever you are.
Does my code go through Trommi?
No. Your agents work on your machine as before. Trommi carries only what an agent sends to your board, and that sealed.
